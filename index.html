The Science of Progressive Overload

To build strength and muscle, your body requires a stimulus that increases over time. This principle is called progressive overload. By adding weight, performing an extra repetition, or perfecting your form, you force your muscles to adapt and grow.

RepTrack helps you capture every detail of your lift sessions so you can easily compare today's performance with last week's. Without tracking, you are guessing. With RepTrack, you are executing a plan.

Built for Strength & Consistency

  • PR Spotlights: Automatically highlight your personal records (PRs) as you hit them, giving you immediate motivation to push your limits.
  • Clean History: Review your past workouts through a structured, easy-to-read chronological feed.
